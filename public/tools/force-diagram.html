    How to use

    1. Sketch the situation in the State sketch pad for one moment in time.
    2. Pick a force type button, then drag outward from the dot. The arrow starts at the dot.
    3. Click an arrow to select it. Drag its shaft to rotate it, or drag its tip to change length and direction.
    4. Use the popover to change the type, tag the object that exerts the force, or rename the label. Type F_T to get a subscript.
    5. Type an underscore for a subscript, so F_T shows a small T. Only the first underscore makes a subscript.
    6. Turn on Snap for 15 degree steps (measured from the tilted +x axis whenever the axes are tilted). Hold Shift to skip snapping. 1D locks each arrow to the +x or +y axis, whichever is closer to your drag.
    7. Turn on Axes to see +x and +y. Tilt them with the round handle on +x or the +15 and -15 buttons in the System panel, then draw arrows parallel and perpendicular to a ramp.
    8. Name your system, turn on Encircle system, and drag the dashed ring around your object(s) on the sketch pad. The force diagram shows only your system. List the external objects that interact with it.
    9. To draw several arrows of one type, Shift+click its button to pin it. Press Escape or click the button again to unpin.

    This is a sandbox. It does not check your answer, so you decide what belongs in your diagram.
