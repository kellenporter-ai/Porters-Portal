Electricity, Light, and the Texas Blackout

Why did the Texas power grid fail in February 2021? Students trace energy through sources, power plants, and the grid.

Lesson 1The Texas blackout (eliciting)

Reading EN/ES

Texas Blackout Article Collection

porters-portal.web.app/texas-blackout-articles

Six short bilingual articles about the 2021 Texas blackout, written at student reading level. Drives the anchoring phenomenon discussion.

Jigsaw-ready: assign different articles to different groups.

Simulation

Texas Grid Blackout Simulation

porters-portal.web.app/texas-grid-blackout

Students balance electricity supply and demand on a model of the Texas grid during the February 2021 freeze, and watch what happens when demand outruns supply.

Lesson 2Power strip safety and circuits

Reading EN/ES

Power Strip Safety Reading

porters-portal.web.app/power-strip-reading

Five-section bilingual reading: complete circuits, circuit breakers, insulation, ground wires, and light bulbs. Pairs with the power strip dissection investigation.

Sections can be assigned independently.

Lesson 3Undergrounding power lines

Reading EN/ES

Undergrounding Power Lines Reading

porters-portal.web.app/undergrounding-reading

Three-section bilingual reading on the 2003 Ohio blackout, why above-ground lines fail, and the engineering tradeoffs of burying power lines.

Lesson 4Energy sources and reliability

Reference EN/ES

Energy Sources Reference

porters-portal.web.app/energy-sources

Digital version of the 8 energy source cards (coal, biomass, natural gas, solar, nuclear, hydro, wind, geothermal). Full card text, quick facts, power plant diagrams, and a sortable comparison table.

Use when printed cards are unavailable, or for review and absent students.

Interactive EN/ES

L4 Blackout Detective — Case Board

porters-portal.web.app/l4-blackout-detective

Students pin evidence to a case board and figure out which energy source's failure cascaded into the blackout. Data-analysis driven.

Interactive EN/ES

L4 Decisions Matrix

porters-portal.web.app/l4-decisions-matrix

Groups rank the energy sources by criteria they choose (reliability, renewability, output), then defend their ranking. Works fully offline once loaded.

Ranking recomputes locally per group; no login or network needed during class.

Reference EN/ES

Grid Reference — Key Concepts & Texas 2021

porters-portal.web.app/grid-reference.html

One-page bilingual reference covering the key vocabulary and concepts of the unit (grid, demand, supply, capacity factor) anchored to the Texas 2021 timeline.
