Motion diagram and change in velocityWeeks 5 to 7

Tap the track to drop velocity dots through the braking phase. Then build the change in velocity, delta-v, by subtracting the earlier velocity from the later one. The arrow shows the direction of that subtraction, not the cart's motion.

Tap the strip to add a dot. Rough placement is fine. What matters is which way your dots grow and which way they shrink.
