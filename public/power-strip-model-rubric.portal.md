# P.1 Lesson 2: Power Strip Model Rubric

## Question 1: Part A, Key Structures
| Skill | Missing | Emerging | Approaching | Developing | Refining |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I am able to identify key components in the power strip and recognize their purpose. | There is no attempt to identify parts of the power strip or their purpose. | There is an attempt to identify parts of the power strip but the parts named are mostly outside the strip (only the wall outlet, the cord, or the devices are mentioned) or the parts are named without any sense of what they do. | There is a partial attempt to identify parts of the power strip because at least one part inside the strip is named but the purpose of most parts is missing, vague, or restated as "it gives power" without explaining how. | There is a solid attempt to identify parts of the power strip because parts inside the strip are named along with parts you can see from outside and a purpose is given for most of the named parts but the purposes may be loosely worded, or the list leaves out a part that matters for sending energy to more than one device. | There is a strong attempt to identify parts of the power strip because visible parts and internal parts are both named with a clear purpose for each and the purposes add up to a story of how energy can be sent from one source to several devices at once. |

## Question 2: Part B, Model Diagram
| Skill | Missing | Emerging | Approaching | Developing | Refining |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I am able to create a model which shows how electrical energy flows within the power strip to power at least two devices. | There is no attempt to draw a model of the power strip. | There is an attempt to draw a model but the drawing shows only the outside of the power strip or only one device or there is no visible path for energy to travel from the source to any device. | There is a partial attempt to draw a model because the drawing shows the source and at least one device and some labels are present but the energy path does not reach every device shown, or the labels name objects without showing how they connect. | There is a solid attempt to draw a model because the drawing shows one energy source connected to the power strip and the path from the strip reaches at least two devices and most key parts are labeled but the flow direction is missing or unclear at some points, or one important internal part is unlabeled or missing. | There is a strong attempt to draw a model because the drawing shows one energy source flowing through the power strip and the path clearly branches to power at least two devices and visible and internal parts are labeled and arrows or another clear device shows the direction the energy travels. |
